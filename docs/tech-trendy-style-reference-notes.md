## Five-aspect grammar for new shots

- **Subject:** original host, fellow travelers when story-relevant, aircraft, food and travel places; switch deliberately between presenter and details.
- **Subject motion:** concrete interaction in temporal order—open suite door, place bag, operate a control, receive and taste food, respond, then act on the next need. No presentation pose without purpose.
- **Scene:** airport, cabin or location with actual scene-appropriate time and weather. Keep overlays—route, elapsed time, factual cost—separate from the setting and legible but economical.
- **Spatial framing:** mix large establishing views, medium host/cabin, POV hands and close product or meal details; preserve geography and object positions.
- **Camera:** choose shot-specific eye-level selfie, forward handheld, controlled detail or exterior view. Playback and transitions serve action; a true continuous physical turn must be seen, while unrelated coverage gets an honest cut. Source sample frames alone cannot prove camera movement or lens choice.

A useful beat is anticipation → physical attempt → result → specific judgment → consequence or next decision. Generate new story turns and original language, and check timing against the story's actual local-time and weather ledger. Refer to the project analyses for timestamps, provenance and coverage limits.
