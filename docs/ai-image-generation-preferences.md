# AI image generation preferences

Updated explicitly by the owner on 2 November 2026. Faster production is a primary
goal alongside quality, realism, exact character identity and continuity.

## Provider order across AI video styles

Use registered **Kie Nano Banana 2.1 (`nano-banana-2-1`) first** for image generation
and editing in all AI video styles, including AI movies, Vlog Style and AI Videos
Documentary Style. A production may use Kie exclusively. This supersedes the earlier
Codex-first rule and separate AI-documentary initial paid-image fallback approval.
The owner authorizes this provider choice within an actually authorized production
and its stated budget; the preference alone does not start a new production.

Use included Codex image generation as an available fallback for ordinary Kie
unavailability or technical failure. Never retry, reword or switch providers to
bypass a safety rejection. Never buy credits, top-ups or subscriptions, use another
paid image provider or exceed the user's actual budget without approval.

## Resolution, speed and review

For 720p/768p video, default to landscape 16:9 1K PNG; use 2K only where cropping or
detail justifies it. Verify returned pixels and current provider prices. Preserve
the exact selected character using identity references, including scratch rebuilds.
Review hands, physical contact, geography, material texture, weather and adjacent
states before animation. Use a few genuinely needed reviewed state masters and
exact native outgoing video frames for continuations; avoid a separately generated
image for every shot and avoid near-duplicate variants. Parallelize independent
ready work with bounded concurrency and one shared budget ledger.
