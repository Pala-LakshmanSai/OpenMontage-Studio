## What the references do that the sample only partly achieves

### 1. A mechanism occupies several shots, rather than one illustrated paragraph

**Direct reference observation:** Nepal's secondary-disaster passage first establishes the constricted channel around 7:29–7:31; then shows the actual mixture of ice, logs and rock around 7:33–7:43. A section-like view exposes the accumulation around 7:45–7:49. Water and obstruction remain the subject around 7:59–8:11, with a brief arrow illustration around 8:03–8:05. Human effort appears around 8:23–8:31, followed by held-back water, failure and a returning destructive flow around 8:33–8:51. The chain diagram around 8:59–9:01 comes **after** the component images have given its symbols meaning.

The transcript explicitly connects accumulation, stored water, downstream exposure and breach. Its dates, casualty counts and claimed institutional findings are not verified by this creative study. The craft is the persistence of one changing mechanism; its factual assertions are not imported.

**Direct reference observation:** asteroid imagery around 5:05–5:19 develops a turbulent surface. Around 5:21–5:35 the view approaches and enters a cavity, around 5:37–5:51 the interior narrows/darkens, and around 5:53–6:19 a rising water column develops and descends. The changing shape, water direction and viewpoint explain a sequence of states. The transcript explains inward convergence followed by upward rebound. The visualization is not established as a quantitatively valid physical simulation.

**Our gap, script/plan evidence:** C11–C13 correctly distinguish fallout, soot, atmospheric height and climate uncertainty, but move through several concept pictures without a single clearly legible atmospheric experiment. Parent final-film observation at **5:42–5:50**: a mushroom-shaped cap appears at an atmospheric layer while narration concerns lofted fire soot. It substitutes the visual icon of a detonation for the mechanism of soot transport.

**Portable change:** before submission, identify a mechanism's initial state, active cause, visible transformation and resulting constraint. Give the same process enough connected coverage to be understood. For an atmospheric explanation, keep one ground fire and a consistent vertical air column; show lower rain removal and a separate contingent higher path. A clean locally annotated layer diagram may reveal the distinction. Do not replace the mechanism with a recognizable disaster silhouette. These fields belong in ordinary staging, not a new review service.

### 2. The image has to show the cause the sentence claims

**Reference strength:** the new Nepal samples show material obstructing a narrow route before water accumulates; the asteroid sequence shows inward flow before a rebound. Their relationship to the transcript is stronger than a generic picture of water during a sentence about pressure.

**Concrete sample failure, parent full-film review:** at **4:10–4:18**, the hand turns the tap off while narration attributes loss of service to damaged infrastructure. The action is mechanically intelligible but explains the wrong cause. My earlier brief C09 check verified a rotary grip and changing water stream; that was insufficient semantic evidence. Correct anatomy and control use do not establish that the picture supports the spoken claim.

**Portable change:** write both “what visibly causes this result” and “what the viewer must not infer caused it.” For infrastructure failure, establish a fully open tap and an untouched control while flow independently weakens; the person notices, checks, or waits afterward. Alternatively show the supply-side failure first and a connected downstream result. Do not let a hand operate a control in the causal window unless that operation is the intended explanation. This generalizes to closing a valve while claiming drought, switching off a lamp while claiming grid failure, or removing a food tray while claiming shortage.

### 3. A comparison teaches; a disclaimer alone does not

**Direct reference observation:** asteroid 1:20–3:20 contrasts differently sized objects and consequences. Around 4:20–4:50, an ordinary ripple and fracture analogy reset intuition before the larger impact process. Around 7:00–7:30, a side view and spreading rings accompany the transcript's distinction between wave-generation mechanisms. Around 9:40–10:30, shore/shelf/island imagery makes local geography part of the explanation.

The transcript repeatedly takes a plausible intuitive model, changes one variable, and derives a different result. It also contains overconfident or questionable factual statements; neither its scale thresholds nor casualty ranking are adopted here without primary research.

**Our gap:** qualifications recur in C02, C03, C06, C12, C13, C15, C17 and C20. They are scientifically responsible, but several occupy the dramatic position where a distinct demonstration could teach the uncertainty. Our script sometimes says that circumstances matter without giving the viewer a visible pair of circumstances.

**Portable change:** state the counterfactual once clearly, use conditional wording at each dependent claim, and reserve a later uncertainty passage for a genuine synthesis. Where possible, show two controlled qualitative cases: identical geography with different wind direction; lower smoke washed out versus an explicitly conditional higher plume; an intact local field with a missing shipment versus a directly damaged field. Do not invent numerical outputs. The comparison should answer why the consequence varies, rather than repeat that nobody can provide one number.

### 4. Scale changes should preserve the question

**Direct reference observation:** Nepal moves from high terrain to rock/ice detail around 1:30–2:00, to inhabitants and pilgrims around 2:10, then route and water-level views around 5:10–6:40. The material and downstream route remain connected. Asteroid shifts from planet/ocean/rock in the opening to impact geometry, coastal settlement around 8:40, ports around 9:10–9:30, then network exposure around 12:50–15:00.

**Our partial success:** the script spans blast, medical care, water, atmosphere, crops, ports, fisheries and surviving communities. That is a useful causal scope. The repeated broad fields and ports, however, can function as category illustrations instead of evidence that one failure changed another system.

**Portable change:** choose one recurring illustrative place or object for each causal chain and carry its condition forward. For food, a grain field, storage bin, delivery route and bakery can be recognizable stages of one dependency. Preserve the same sack/bin/route cue when revisited. Cut wide to show where the constraint lives, close to show what physically changes, and back to people to show the consequence. A new setting must answer the current question or introduce a causally new one. This does not require one traveling host or a fabricated real town.

### 5. Human stakes are concrete before they become large

**Direct reference observation:** Nepal inserts a small recovered shoe on a work surface around **9:50**, a helmet and footwear around **10:40**, isolated roads/helicopter access around **11:00–11:10**, waiting people around **17:00–17:10**, and rebuilding around **17:20**. The actual provenance of those reconstructed-looking images and the narrated victim claims is unknown. What is observable is a deliberate change from large landscape to an ordinary personal object or achievable action.

Asteroid's boat occupants around **4:10** make scale visible; its ports, harvest, warehouse and factory passages around **12:50–14:50** make distant dependency tangible. They do not need a speaking host to give the mechanism a human consequence.

**Our gap:** hospital supplies, an ambulance and the C18 water container provide useful human-scale material, but most social effects remain broad categories. The loaf is the most memorable object and therefore must carry a precise meaning.

**Parent final-film observation:** the opening says a table has no food while showing an abundant loaf. The ending shows full bread again without a sufficiently legible visual change from the hypothetical aftermath back to present reality. Visual continuity alone is not a resolved callback.

**Portable change:** establish the ordinary object, show the threatened or absent state when the script claims it, and earn the final reframe. An empty plate during the specific no-food line, followed by a clearly marked present-day return to the opening table, communicates the premise without pretending recovery occurred. Alternatively change the wording so the intact loaf is explicitly the thing at risk. Never use a symbolic object in a way that contradicts the literal sentence. Avoid invented dead victims, quoted witnesses or false reportage to make the film emotional.

### 6. Vary explanatory tempo, not just camera movement

**Direct observation:** Nepal combines short material/diagram inserts with broader landscape and human consequence views. Asteroid allows visibly evolving ocean/space processes more room, then changes representational mode. Its calm opening sea and later violent cavity are different information states; both are not simply a slow moving view.

**Measured sample evidence from parent:** every final interval is at least 5.7917 seconds, most exceed eight seconds, and every thirty-second source chunk was retained. That shows little short-insert contrast. It does not alone prove boredom, but it explains why the sample can feel uniformly measured despite many topic changes. The reference detector's old mean lengths are not reliable enough for an exact cross-video ratio.

**Portable change:** vary duration by information. A brief 3–5-second material insert can establish the critical contact; a 6–12-second process can show a meaningful transformation; a short quiet aftermath can let its result register. These are original planning options, not measured channel quotas. Preserve the owner's actual two-second minimum. Never force every thought into the same 30-second three-shot rhythm. Keep useful 30-second generation requests for capacity efficiency, but let them contain a deliberately varied edit and complete native utterances. Trim genuinely redundant picture only when speech and target length remain coherent; never slow narration to use the whole job.

## Sound: preserve the provenance distinction

The saved full transcripts repeatedly contain music tags. Previously authorized external low-resolution AI reports describe restrained low-register narration, dark ambient/synth underscore, low-frequency rumble and water/material effects, with music retreating under speech and rising around spectacle. Those are **model-reported qualitative descriptions**, not my own listening. Do not preserve their exact dB, frequency, WPM, accent, instrument or pause claims as measurements. The reports also contain incorrect visual details; certainty wording is not evidence.

Our generation prompts explicitly requested no music and quiet synchronized ambience. That is a documented production-choice difference; this re-review does not establish exactly what the final mix sounds like. The saved style permits score, but the pass does not authorize a separate paid music provider.

Portable refinement: plan a coherent sound progression by sequence role—ordinary environment, mechanism, impact, aftermath, explanation, final landing—within the authorized audio route. Keep one narrator identity with varied intention. Do not restart a separate dramatic bed at every generation boundary or use a loud impact for each cut. If a score is authorized and available, choose one coherent editorial arc; if unavailable, intentionally use ambience/dynamic space rather than pretending metadata proves reference parity. New spending or cloned narration is not implied.

## Reference defects that must not become style rules

Nepal's official-looking documents and newspaper contain malformed text, including an apparent disease-risk report around14:40 and newspaper around15:10. The denser mechanism re-review also shows a supposedly improvised debris barrier acquiring conspicuous concrete side structures around8:33–8:45. That is a visual/claim inconsistency worth avoiding, not a prompt to add more engineering detail. The script's casualty totals, dates, institutional claims and numerical comparisons are unverified here.

Asteroid uses dramatic giant-wave and globe/cloud imagery that cannot be treated as measured simulation output. Its transcript contains strong outcome statements and factual claims outside this creative review's verification. Copy neither its apparent scientific certainty nor a literal global disaster icon. Full-frame output and the owner's two-second minimum also override reference letterboxing and any short source fragments.

The better reusable target is **a visible causal explanation with controlled comparisons, persistent material states, specific human consequences and a resolved callback**. It is more concrete than “cinematic footage, serious narration and a disaster topic.” Apply it while writing and staging; this explicit owner-requested re-study does not establish a routine full-reference rewatch, per-clip paid review or new approval gate.
