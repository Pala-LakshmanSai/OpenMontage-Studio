Verified 3 November 2026. References:
[Lituya Bay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nhaTh5gGXiM) and

This is original analytical commentary, not copied narration or independent

## Coverage and limits

Both downloaded sources were 640×360 landscape copies. Local `VideoAnalyzer`
completed metadata, scene detection, optical-flow classification, keyframes and
audio-energy analysis. Its local-source run did not obtain a transcript; the
separately supplied channel transcripts were read in full. No paid analysis was
performed by this study. Audio-energy computation does not establish audible
music, voice quality, sound design or intelligibility.

Personally inspected every frame on all generated contact sheets: **370 sampled
frames across 11 sheets for Lituya; 568 across 16 sheets for humans disappear**,
plus each video's near-final frame. Coverage combines every six seconds through
the whole timeline, every half-second for the opening minute, every detected
segment midpoint, pairs around detected cuts in the first 90 seconds, and every
two seconds through the final minute. Thus this is dense sampled full-timeline
visual review, **not continuous/full-speed viewing or audible listening**. It
cannot certify HD quality, every motion artifact or exact shot counts.

The durable [coverage manifest](evidence/the-last-day-20261103/visual-b-coverage.json)
records every reviewed timestamp. Temporary sheets, individual frames and analyzer
briefs are under `/tmp/the-last-day-study/visual-b/<video_id>/`; these are working
evidence, not portable dependencies for future production. Reuse these notes
without requiring the temporary files.

The FFmpeg detector misses visible changes: Lituya changes from boats to stars
between 9.0–9.5 seconds, and lookout to a giant wave between 36.5–37.0, inside
larger detected segments. Humans-disappear similarly has multiple visible cuts

### Story mechanics and ending

The reference suspends the immediate boat decision while teaching the viewer
why the place is dangerous. Geography, prior warning signs and the fault supply
the cause. It then returns to the dated event, separates trigger from mechanism,
uses a household-scale physical analogy, establishes the measurement evidence,
and pays off the human question with three different boat outcomes. Finally it
widens the relevance to similar settings and asks the viewer to imagine the
decision. The CTA remains related to the opening dilemma rather than changing
to an unrelated channel promotion.

Rehooks are changes in the question: why this apparently safe place is dangerous;
why the earthquake alone is not the main disaster; why the exceptional height is
credible; what the trapped captain can do; why some boats survive and another
does not; whether the mechanism could recur. The narration carries most of this
structure. The pictures are explanatory coverage and reconstructed set pieces,
not a dialogue-led continuous character drama.

### Visual strengths and defects to improve

- Cold blue/gray water, subdued green land and warm cabin practicals keep an
  ominous, realistic-looking palette. Humans and practical objects provide
  scale after landscape spectacle; macro chain detail makes the decision legible.
- Many images recur almost unchanged: starry boat grouping, sleeping cabin,
  giant curtain-wave, curled wave, surveyor, offshore boat/chain and scoured
  ground. Repetition helps recall but sometimes supplies illustration without
  new information. Improve by preserving a motif while advancing its state.
- The bay's geography is not consistently one location: vertical canyon walls,
  forested fjords, broad blue coast and rocky tapering formations alternate.
  Daylight, starry sky, dawn and bright wave shots also alternate within the
  reconstruction. Future films should distinguish explanatory diagrams/other
  locations from the specific continuous event and maintain its light/geography.
- Some giant-water imagery reads as a vertical curtain or curling ocean breaker
  rather than a physically precise run-up demonstration. The Eiffel Tower
  standing in the bay is an analogy, not event footage. Mark illustrative scale
  comparisons clearly and separately from reconstructed evidence.
- The transcript's opening family description differs from its later boat
  roster; do not propagate an unverified roster into a factual reconstruction.
  Source-check all dates, casualties, dimensions, personal accounts and mechanics.
- Optical flow labeled 76 detected spans motion, four static, one animated
  still. This supports a video-led treatment; it is not proof of exact media
  provenance or a requirement that every shot be generated. Dense samples show
  actual independent subject/water movement, including man rising and dog turning.

## Humans disappear — 19:18.63

### Opening and pacing

The first 0–13 seconds preview structural failure: flooded building base, rusty
support close-up, skyline, a tower falling into the water, then an aerial dust
cloud. Detected boundaries at 1.367 and 5.133 seconds are visible in bracketing
samples; additional visible changes occur around 3 and 10 seconds. The initial
1.367-second shot conflicts with the owner's normal two-second rule.

By 13 seconds the picture has returned to a lit sunset/night city. The sequence
then narrows to a lit window (16.333), power station (19.866), transmission lines
(visible by 23.367), analog meters (26.867), control displays (30.3), city lights
(by 34.5), long-exposure roadway (by 42), Earth at night (by 45.5), empty street

and a conceptual timeline (around 68–72). This is **a short destructive preview
followed by a roughly minute-long premise explanation**, not a single homogeneous

The opening makes ordinary infrastructure newly interesting by exposing the
maintenance it depends on. Its rhetorical reversal is that abandonment would
not preserve a quiet museum. The time ladder promises a destination: the last
human trace. This creates forward movement without a protagonist or survivor.

### Story mechanics, graphics and continuity

Each time jump changes the governing mechanism: electricity and pumps, animals
and fires, moisture/freeze-thaw, corrosion, roots/rivers, structural failure,
burial and geological preservation. This is more effective than repeating
progressively greener city images. The proof pattern is usually **system or
material, physical process, visible consequence, larger implication**. Familiar
objects (a gutter, bridge cable or painted steel) make abstract time tangible.

The late turn changes the question from which buildings remain to what effects
cannot be reversed. The final reflection returns to the initial maintenance
premise; the CTA asks the viewer to choose what might last and points to a related
extinction topic. A newly imagined animal ignoring a remnant around 1118–1127
seconds gives the abstract legacy a memorable visual form.

Chapter headings are large centered **white uppercase serif**, unlike Lituya's
condensed sans-serif type-on. Clearly observed examples are MONTH 1, YEAR 1,
YEAR 5 and YEAR 20. Do not assert every spoken chapter has a matching card.
Graphics are substantive here: a supply/load balance, network plan, map, line
graph, root/soil section, structural material demonstration, city grid and
geological layers. They explain invisible systems, not merely decorate the frame.
This reference therefore does not support a blanket “no graphics” style rule.

No persistent word-by-word subtitles were visible in reviewed samples. Internal
gauges, screens and graph labels sometimes look malformed or semantically weak;
the timeline around 68–72 seconds has visibly unreliable tiny words. A future
film should use deliberately authored, factual explanatory graphics and legible
surface text. Do not imitate generated nonsense labels.

Visual motion is mixed. The automatic classifier labels 115 detected spans
motion, 79 static and 14 animated-still, but its cut misses and thresholding
make these approximate diagnostics. Some samples are near-static illustrations
or slow camera movement; other passages clearly animate collapse, waves, fire,
growth, animals and water. Preserve motion for causal transformations instead
of assuming a continuous live-action simulation throughout.

The film intercuts many cities, climates and illustrative time compressions.
This works as a global counterfactual, but matching a landmark's before/after
state would give an original film stronger continuity. Human maintenance inserts
must be explicitly legible as comparisons to the inhabited world; otherwise
they can confuse the vanished-human premise. The 300-second maintenance image
uses grayscale, whereas some other human/active infrastructure inserts are less
clearly separated. Speculative creatures and distant-future observers also need
to remain clearly hypothetical, not scientific predictions.

## Transfer into the reusable style

Keep narrated causal explanation, scale changes, concrete material mechanisms,
calm-to-threat contrast, occasional purposeful explanatory graphics and an ending
that changes the meaning of the opening. Choose the structure by title: historical
reconstruction with human stakes, or hypothetical timeline with system stakes.
Do not force a fictional speaking protagonist into the second form.

Use shorter purposeful opening setup when appropriate under owner preference,
but distinguish that adaptation from measured reference timing. Preserve at least
two seconds for ordinary actual shots and meaningful screen states. Improve
geography/state consistency, factual sourcing, legible graphics and useful visual
variety rather than copying reference glitches or replaying the same filler shot.
Narrator and score architecture should be reconciled with the separate audio
study; this visual study establishes neither voice character nor soundtrack mix.
