## What the format actually is

The shared core is **narrated causal explanation with cinematic illustrations**.
It combines an intriguing premise, physical mechanisms, changes in scale, human
or systemic consequences and a thematic landing. It is not simply an apocalypse
montage, a dialogue-led AI movie, a selfie vlog or a silent observational film.

The five references use distinct structures:

| Reference | Opening promise | Middle engine | Ending function |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nepal | Immediate violent footage, then a dated catastrophe and recurrence question | Geology, first flow, debris dam, second surge, rescue/logistics and new hazard | Returns to mountain/lake danger; unresolved risk, no mandatory CTA |
| Moon | Dramatized emergency, then ordinary orbital motion becomes an impossible scenario | Explicit changed rule, countdown, nonuniform acceleration, tides, intervention limits, disruption/impact | Aftermath, origin reversal, actual lunar recession and ordinary watcher callback |
| Asteroid | The apparently safer ocean is challenged | Scale, high-speed impact, cavity/rebound, wave myth correction, coastline exposure, atmosphere and supply chains | Historical comparison, detection/prevention, answer and future-topic invitation |
| Lituya | Quiet boats become a trapped human decision under extraordinary scale | Geography, fault/rockfall mechanism, run-up comparison, different boat outcomes, physical evidence | Recurrence relevance and a viewer question linked to the opening decision |
| Humans disappear | Ordinary light hides continuous maintenance; preview signals its failure | Time ladder with a different mechanism at each stage, from grids/pumps to rust/roots to burial | What persists changes from buildings to ecological/geological effects; opening premise reframed |

The consistent attention device is a **change in understanding**. The audience
first expects one outcome; the explanation reveals a less obvious mechanism or
vulnerability. The new information creates the next question. The title's visible
spectacle is delivered, then its implications receive time rather than an abrupt
ending immediately after destruction.

## Writing, rehooks and ending design

Use concrete nouns, physical verbs, familiar scale comparisons and understandable
technical language. The reference transcripts frequently turn from a seemingly
reassuring observation to its hidden dependency or cost. Repetition emphasizes
a crucial number or changes an earlier statement's meaning; it is not a license
to repeat the same warning with different catastrophe footage.

Each major section should carry: current question, new mechanism/evidence,
visible consequence, what now becomes vulnerable, and next question. An answer
must arrive before another long chain of promises accumulates. The most useful
reversals move from direct impact to indirect systems, present crisis to long-term
aftermath, or physical scale to a human choice. They need not all be dramatic
character decisions; an abandoned-world film has no surviving protagonist.

Endings are topic-specific. Copying a single cliffhanger onto every film would
miss the channel's variation. Record the opening promise and its final answer,
the last conceptual turn, visual callback and brief optional CTA. Reality checks
matter particularly for impossible counterfactuals; historical films need
documented outcomes and evidence rather than manufactured certainty.

## Five-aspect synthesis for production

| Aspect | Recurring observation | Original production rule |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | Natural systems, infrastructure, vulnerable people, materials, instruments, astronomical objects | Identify the exact subject and its role in the explanation; a host is not required. |
| Subject Motion | Falling mass, flowing water, cracking/corrosion, growth, activity and physical demonstrations; mixed quiet/still passages | Show the mechanism's action and result, with credible mass/contact/time, not a pan over unrelated scenery. |
| Scene | Geography/space, ordinary places, work/research rooms, reconstructions and explanatory models; overlays vary by topic | Separate illustration, diagram, factual evidence and hypothesis; retain recurring world state. |
| Spatial Framing | Extreme scale alternates with macro material, human medium and geographical aerial | Make scale changes answer the narration; anchor huge consequences in a familiar object/place. |
| Camera | Restrained drift/travel, aerial/orbital-like approaches, fixed process views, occasional rough witness-looking opener | Use purposeful movement and stable geometry; do not prescribe invented exact lenses or one continuous camera. |

Nepal's shorter explanatory montage and asteroid's longer process passages show
that pacing is adaptable. Automated segment means and motion labels vary greatly
and sometimes disagree with visible cuts. Do not publish exact shot quotas,
still/video percentages or mean edit lengths from these heuristics. Use complete
action/thought, information density and the owner's minimum actual2s rule.

## Visual finish, graphics and packaging

The inspected imagery often uses blue/gray water/space/mountains, warm motivated
interiors or heat, large silhouettes, depth and close material texture. Climate,
light and geography are not perfectly consistent in the references; improve those
in the new work. Deliberate explanatory/time changes remain legitimate montage.

Typography is not one uniform package: Lituya has condensed sans-serif date/place
type-on; humans-disappear has large centered white serif time headings; Moon has
a distressed bold day label; asteroid has comparatively sparse visible typography.
Maps, cross-sections, networks, comparisons and instrument screens explain things
ordinary footage cannot show. No persistent karaoke-caption treatment was observed.
Author critical text locally; retain legible accurate units, axis and scale.

Thumbnail inspection shows large short white uppercase headings, one dominant
threat or changed environment, clear small-versus-large scale and, in three of
the five, yellow directional arrows. The text often frames a question, location
or elapsed time rather than repeating the entire title. The palette favors strong
dark/light contrast. This is packaging grammar, not evidence of CTR causation.
Use original composition and branding if a thumbnail is requested. Some references
exaggerate the threat or ecology; the new thumbnail must match the film's actual
premise, not imply a scientifically unsupported giant animal or false event.

Source letterboxing and occasional sub-two-second clips are not copied. Owner
rules require full-frame landscape720p/768p and actual ordinary shots≥2s. Preserve
explanatory graphics when useful rather than imposing a no-text/no-graphics ban.

## Audio: qualitative model observations, not exact measurements

Across five external analyses, the recurring reported direction is a mature,
low-register offscreen narrator with clear formal diction, restrained seriousness,
and intentional pauses. Accent labels differ, so no exact accent is established.
All describe cinematic ambient/synth/orchestral support, stronger energy around
major consequences and quieter reflection, with water/wind/metal/ice/impact
effects. Humans-disappear additionally reports urban ambience giving way to
wildlife. Nepal and Moon include brief dramatized/source-like speech at the
opening or an inserted scene; the others are principally narration.

These findings inform original sound direction. They do not identify a voice
provider, demonstrate a cloned identity, or establish exact WPM, frequency bands,
ducking offsets, pause lengths or consistently perfect mix quality. Use the
actual selected voice's timing. Do not inherit uniformly slow delivery, vocals
in score or unrequested news impersonation. Maintain narration intelligibility,
intentional dynamic variation and coherent ambience across generation chunks.

## What to improve rather than imitate

- **Factual reliability:** catalogue/chapters sometimes contradict runtimes or
  their own topic; Lituya's narration has a family/boat-roster discrepancy. Treat
  reference scientific, geographic and casualty claims as unverified until sourced.
- **False evidence:** documents/monitors have malformed labels; cinematic officials,
  rescue scenes and charts cannot substantiate real testimony or measurements.
- **Physics/scale:** some waves resemble curling surf or vertical curtains, lunar
  apparent size changes, and material motion sometimes looks synthetic. Stage
  clear causal processes and use restrained accurate diagrams for critical science.
- **Continuity:** track recognizable terrain, structures, time/light and cumulative
  damage. Recurring imagery should develop a motif rather than pad runtime.
- **Text and graphics:** purposeful explanatory images are valuable; gibberish,
  meaningless monitors and false quantitative precision are not.
- **Production overhead:** research is reusable. This requested deep study is not
  a default mandate for dense contact sheets, per-clip ASR or paid reviews on films.

External reports also overclaim. Requested resolution was LOW regardless of their
claims of native1080p/continuous playback. They disagree with local endings and
shot details in places, contain overlapping timeline ranges, and sometimes label
events factual/speculative merely from a date. Local coverage takes precedence;
no scientific claim becomes verified because the report calls it verified.

## Three original treatment patterns

These illustrate how the style changes with a title; they are not new film orders.

1. **Counterfactual countdown:** an ordinary system loses one defined property;
   trace initial invisibility, first measurable failure, systemic escalation,
   intervention limits and a real-world grounding. Example direction: a clearly
   labeled thought experiment about losing magnetic shielding, with effects
   independently researched rather than assumed instant extinction.
2. **Historical causal investigation:** open at a documented human dilemma,
   reconstruct the setting/mechanism, distinguish multiple outcomes, show the
   physical evidence and end with the specific lesson. Original writing/casting,
   no invented official quotations or copied survivor dialogue.
3. **Long-time transformation:** begin with an overlooked maintenance dependency,
   move through increasingly long intervals with new physical mechanisms, then
   ask what leaves a lasting trace. Keep time ranges conditional on environment.
