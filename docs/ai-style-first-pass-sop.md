## Outcome and spending authority

Aim for an excellent, complete first delivery. Invest reasoning, reference study,
script work and preparation before generation instead of relying on paid rerolls
or a second production pass. A technically valid file is not editorial acceptance.

When an authorized production requires corrections for quality, autonomously use
justified paid retakes, replacements, second passes or necessary fallbacks within
the user's actual budget and scope. Diagnose the defect, choose a targeted path,
estimate and record incremental cost and avoid waste or blind retry loops. Follow
provider-specific initial image-fallback authorization in
[AI image preferences](ai-image-generation-preferences.md). Do not treat a forecast
reserve as independent spending authority or invent a budget cap. Never exceed a
user-set budget or buy credits, top-ups or subscriptions without approval.

Preserve quality: never disguise a failed take, weaken the brief or omit essential
scenes to avoid a repair. Generative outputs are stochastic; preparation reduces
preventable failures but does not guarantee first-pass success. Preserve accepted
assets and repair only what needs correction. If a required action would exceed a
user-set budget or require a credit purchase or unauthorized initial provider
fallback, preserve progress and request the required authorization.

## Before paid generation

For an explicitly selected Frameo unlimited pass, apply
[rolling production](ai-movie-frameo-production.md) and
[shared unlimited policy](unlimited-plan-production-preferences.md). Resolve the
whole-film causal spine, ending, cast/world, runtime allocation and provider/spend
readiness first. Finish detailed script, staging, speech timing and references for
each sequence **before that sequence is submitted**; start ready independent
sequences while later detail is prepared. The full detailed screenplay is not a
global submission barrier. This owner refinement of 3 November 2026 supersedes
the full-script-before-spending wording below for that route. Unresolved local
dependencies hold their own jobs, not unrelated ready work. No speculative filler.

1. Read the exact style instructions, current user training, reference analysis
   and relevant provider skills. Turn them into a concrete acceptance checklist;
   resolve contradictions before spending. Distinguish observed reference details
   from unverified soundtrack, and never invent reference aesthetics or footage balance.
2. Finish the full story/script, causal sequence, duration allocation and shot/edit
   plan. Check dialogue timing, action complexity, reaction space, camera handoffs,
   coverage and payoff. Solve predictable weak shots in the plan. For Frameo
   unlimited, apply the rolling sequence exception above.
3. Lock the film bible and state ledger: identities, voices, costumes, props,
   anatomy/contact, visible and offscreen posture, geography, eyelines, camera
   ownership/axis/lens, light, action phase and each take's start/end state.
   Follow [global environmental continuity](ai-video-global-realism-preferences.md)
   for time of day, daylight, season, climate and weather, and plan plausible
   transitions between times and locations. Track within-day sun direction and
   height, shadows, light, cloud, rain, wetness, wind, haze and ambience across
   consecutive shots; allow plausible progression with local time and weather.
4. Generate/review image masters and anchors through Kie Nano Banana 2.1 first
   within the authorized production budget, across all AI video styles. Codex is
   the ordinary technical-unavailability fallback. This supersedes the separate
   initial paid-image approval rule for AI documentary production. Compare
   neighboring compositions and poses together.
   Inspect faces, hands, props, backgrounds, texture, framing and actual dimensions.
   Reuse accepted continuation frames when appropriate.
5. Match shots to verified model capabilities. Validate schemas/build requests,
   required reference uploads, supported durations/resolutions, dialogue/audio
   conditioning, authentication readiness and local render availability without
   launching disposable paid tests. Resolve unsupported fields and stale routes.
6. Plan continuous native speech, clean ambience/Foley and suitable instrumental
   cues. Check voice conditioning, soundtrack contamination risks, cue durations,
   music billing/rounding and source permissions before committing.
   Follow [AI music preferences](ai-music-preferences.md): Lyria 3 Pro through fal
   for background music, ElevenLabs Music through fal only when precise timing
   is needed. Choose each cue's provider now, not after paying for a failed trial.
7. Forecast first-pass asset counts, billed video duration, reference
   surcharges, music, existing subscription coverage and total provider spend.
   Separate estimates from actual charges. Do not assume image allowance is
   unlimited or authorize paid overflow when it runs out. Under the rolling
   Frameo exception, make the current ready chunks exact and estimate remaining
   coverage; unfinished later shot counts do not block ready independent work.
8. Record a readiness checklist under `artifacts/first-pass-readiness.md`, with
   evidence pointers and unresolved items, alongside canonical pipeline artifacts.
   Hold only work dependent on an unresolved required item; continue ready
   independent sequences under the rolling exception. Do not self-certify checks
   that were not actually performed.
