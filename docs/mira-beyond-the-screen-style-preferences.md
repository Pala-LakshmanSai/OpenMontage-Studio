# Mira Beyond the Screen Style

Initial reusable profile completed 4 November 2026 before sample generation. Named from the channel's actual display name. Apply through `pipeline_defs/cinematic.yaml` and `styles/mira-beyond-the-screen.yaml`. Invoke: “Make [TITLE/TOPIC], approximately [DURATION], in Mira Beyond the Screen Style.”

## What the style does

Build original character-led science-fiction drama. A concrete personal desire pulls a capable but fallible person into a system larger than they understand. Everyday practical behavior makes that person worth following. A discovery changes the interpretation of the mission; attempts encounter opposition and lasting costs. Spectacle enlarges the consequence of a choice. Finish the external problem and the human question, including reaction and aftermath.

The emotional pattern is curiosity and familiarity, unease, active discovery, changed plan, consequential choice, earned scale, and human landing. It is not a mandatory rigid act count. Long films need several developed tension/recovery cycles with different tactics. Two-minute samples need a coherent miniature arc rather than compressed feature exposition.

The underlying engine is **small human promise against vast unfamiliar circumstances**. A meal, tool, message, household responsibility or uneasy joke can establish attachment; a lost route, broken trust or forfeited resource makes escape consequential. Preserve ordinary warmth and wonder alongside danger. Use original events, characters, entities and worlds; never copy Mira's likeness/voice, PIP's design, scripts, logos or footage.

## Title, opening and story

A title gives an understandable person, situation and consequential reversal. Fulfill that promise visibly. Use a compelling anomaly, urgent practical objective or interpersonal friction early; do not add an empty universal catastrophe montage. References sometimes develop ordinary life before danger. The owner's short hook and stronger causal stakes are adaptations, not proof the channel always opens with danger in eight seconds.

Within the opening, establish who is present, where they are, what they want and the first contradiction. Let information arrive through actions, communications and discoveries rather than an omniscient lecture. A clue should alter a decision. Let the audience occasionally understand danger first, while keeping spatial cause readable.

Every sequence changes objective, access, knowledge, trust, cost or relationship. Give supporting characters independent needs. Humorous lines arise from practical friction or imperfect cooperation. Avoid exposition that merely repeats visible information and generic inspirational declarations.

Seed the decisive solution before its use. The climax resolves through a choice and an achievable action, not an unrelated rescue appearing without a prior signal or a new magical capability. Preserve consequences after success. A character's loss or reunion requires reaction; do not erase hurt with one smiling close-up. A complete film must answer its main question; an episode can leave the larger series question open after a local payoff.

## Picture, blocking and camera

Full-frame landscape moving footage at 720p/768p. Establish geography with a useful wide view, dramatize choices through medium relationships, use close reactions for recognition and detail inserts for causal work. People remain legible against huge scale. Preserve solid routes, barrier sides, sight lines and equipment locations. A new lens/angle does not reset physical state.

Design lived-in mechanical interiors with tactile surfaces, purposeful lights, believable controls and modest wear. Alien spaces can contrast through smooth architecture, translucency, scale or biological forms. Palette follows place and dramatic state: warm service light/cold exterior is useful, not compulsory. Faces, contacts and required secondary subjects remain readable when practical story lights dim. Avoid uniform beauty lighting, plastic faces, universal teal/orange grading and detail added solely to look expensive.

Choose camera movement for discovery, action or emotional distance. Temporal samples show restrained relationship coverage and practical-action inserts; they do not establish exact lenses. Use a stable eye-level medium for important speech, a motivated track/pan for travel and an intelligible reveal for scale. No automatic orbit, constant zoom, universal slow motion or forced selfie turn.

For each ready take define Subject, Subject Motion, Scene (overlays separately), Spatial Framing and Camera, plus incoming state, causal result, outgoing state and edit purpose. Bind identity, location and mechanism references by role; exclude stale states. Preserve face/body/clothes, voices, cast count, carried objects, wetness, damage, loss, light and geography. Independent setups use clean original roots; true same-shot continuations use actual outgoing state where supported.

## Editing and rhythm

Observed automatic segment medians range roughly 2.8–5.4 seconds in the fiction set, with substantial longer holds and detection error. Use this as broad context, not a fixed metronome. As a practical starting point, use useful 3–7-second picture beats, longer complete conversations/reactions when earned, and shorter urgency coverage only above the actual two-second floor. Native speech and important actions determine edit points.

Alternate discovery/action and response. A control insert needs a visible result or changed decision; a wide needs to establish access, danger, scale or emotional context. Vary shot sizes and duration with pressure. Cut on meaningful action or attention. Keep complete utterances, breath/reaction room and effect tails. Use sound bridges where useful. Do not retain all thirty generated seconds merely because the provider returned them. Remove next-scene fragments and false-start spillover. Do not use dissolves, crops or darkness to hide contradictions.

Reserve ending space for consequence and emotional release. Finish picture and sound deliberately. Distinguish optional credits/promotional material from the actual story endpoint. Approximate duration is flexible enough to preserve the payoff without padding.

## Speech and sound architecture

Default to original characters speaking in the scene, sparse useful communications/computer voices, physical location sound and purposeful silence. The studied caption evidence supports dialogue-led fiction, not continuous explanatory narration. Narration is optional when a particular story needs it; it is not a shortcut for missing performances. No imported Vlog Style face quota.

Keep one consistent original voice per role while pace/emotion follow circumstances. Write contractions, concrete observations, short complete thought groups and mild practical humor. Bind exact lines to named speakers and shot-local mouth windows. Keep staging prose out of spoken text. Preserve native paired speech/picture; do not dub independently timed TTS over speaking faces and call it synchronized.

Caption markers confirm some references contain music, especially ending passages. Exact instrumentation/mix were not heard. Implementation may use a restrained original background cue that leaves dialogue/Foley space, builds with the costly choice and releases after the outcome; silence is useful. Declare this as a production design, not measured channel instrumentation. For a cue use Lyria 3 Pro through fal.ai; ElevenLabs Music through fal only when exact timing is needed. Keep native ambience/contact effects under the cue and continuous across joins. No constant maximum-intensity trailer score, generic impact on every cut or loud music used to manufacture tension.

If direct listening is unavailable, report that limit and use permitted local waveform/loudness/clipping/transcript checks. These do not establish perceived naturalness or exact soundtrack matching. Do not convert unperformed listening into a passed check.
