# Consistent voices without a production approval gate

Owner override, 3 November 2026: do not stop to ask for voice selection. This
supersedes the 2 November requirement to present options and await an explicit
choice before full spoken-video production. Apply across AI videos with generated
character speech or an approved narrator, including movies and spoken vlog styles.
It does not add speech or narration to a film that is meant to remain unspoken.

Reuse a suitable settled character voice and clean supported reference when one
exists. Otherwise select an appropriate consistent voice autonomously within the
authorized native production route: match the original character's age, accent,
register and intended delivery. Record the choice and continue the authorized
film. Use the first planned useful dialogue take to establish reusable voice
conditioning; do not add a separate audition, voice-ranking call, routine review
or approval stage. A rejected audition is not an accepted voice reference.

Record the character, voice ID or conditioning sentence/reference, accent, age
impression, delivery traits and provider/model in the existing character bible
and decision log. Keep the voice consistent with the immutable selected face.
Prepare situation-led speakable words, action, facial framing and motivated light
through [human performance](ai-video-human-performance-preferences.md). Preserve
complete native speech-picture pairs and use supported controls rather than
promising an exact clone or perfectly identical delivery on every take.

Ask about a voice only when the owner explicitly requests options/approval or
when genuinely required information or rights are missing for a specifically
requested voice. This is not a routine production pause. The removed voice gate
does not change other actual authorization constraints: no unapproved subscription,
budget increase, external TTS/lip-sync replacement or provider safety bypass.
Keep the style's no-narrator/no-speech defaults, supplied original audio, current
budget, fast workflow and accepted/uncertain-job no-replay protections.

Historical glacier notes: the three native H3 voice auditions were rejected as
still artificial. The separate ElevenLabs audition was cancelled before paid
submission and must not be revived without fresh authorization. The current
owner instruction removes the voice-selection wait; it does not retroactively
accept those auditions or change the selected host identity. Native descriptions
and supported audio references guide a performance, not a guaranteed exact clone.
