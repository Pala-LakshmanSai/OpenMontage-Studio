## Main comparison

Our sample has the right subject, an original narrator, a clear physics-to-systems chain, a useful bread callback and unusually careful scenario boundaries. Its weaker structural feature is **twenty similarly sized explanatory units**, each presenting another class of effect. The references more often make an event happen to something already established, pause to explain the mechanism, then return to settle the earlier question. Their recurring images acquire a new meaning; they are not simply recurring decoration.

Lituya carries an unresolved practical situation: recognizable people, a boat, an anchor and an approaching wave. Without-humans carries an unresolved material situation: recognizable infrastructure is actively maintained, loses that protection, deteriorates and leaves a different kind of trace. Neither mechanism requires copying the channel’s characters, language, shots or exact durations.

## Specific revisions our sample’s script suggests

1. **Give the opening one actual interruption.** C01 at 00:00 promises an empty table, while C02–C03 at 00:30–01:30 spend two entire blocks defining rules and qualifications before the first physical mechanism. Keep the impossible premise explicit, but compress its nonnegotiable rules into a brief integrated line and stage one ordinary dependency failing. Do not remove scientific boundaries; give them the moment where they change understanding.
2. **Carry a concrete unresolved state through the explanation.** The bread motif appears in the opening, returns as a section at 07:00 and closes at 09:30. Between those points, water, hospitals, ports, smoke, crops and fish largely replace one another. Choose a primary household/service dependency and two or three returning states. Each return should reveal a new constraint or meaningful response, not simply show the same attractive loaf again.
3. **Let linked shots prove mechanisms.** C09 at 04:00 compresses treatment, power, chemicals, pumps, transport, fuel, communications and the failed tap into one paragraph. This is strong causal thinking but a crowded visual assignment. Select a few decisive links—operating component, changed input, visible output loss, human consequence—and give them complete useful shots. Use an explanatory cutaway when the essential process is otherwise invisible. Lituya’s chain and without-humans’ roof/rebar sequences are the model, not their particular props.
4. **Increase consequential changes, not only topic changes.** C10–C16 move from distance to soot to altitude to climate to crops to trade to oceans. Each is relevant, but a viewer can experience them as another list. A mechanism should overturn the prior apparent answer: an intact home still needs a functioning supply chain; food present somewhere is not food deliverable here; a surviving field is not a guaranteed harvest. Then show a practical consequence. Distinguish illustrative possibilities from predictions for the unmodeled scenario.
5. **Vary chapter size and question size.** Twenty 30-second production packages need not become twenty equal rhetorical chapters. Lituya spends multiple linked shots on one human decision; without-humans lets one mechanism occupy several scales before jumping time. Plan narration and unresolved questions across package boundaries. Preserve complete native speech and actual two-second minimum shots; generation efficiency is not an editorial metronome.
6. **Use uncertainty once globally, then locally when it changes the branch.** The sample repeats related forecast limits at 01:00, 02:30, 05:30, 06:00, 07:00 and 08:00. Their distinctions are valid, but similar disclaimer cadence can reduce forward movement. Establish the overall boundary, then make a later caveat a concrete fork—smoke removed low down versus smoke persisting aloft—whose contrasting consequences the viewer can see. Do not replace honest uncertainty with false confidence.
7. **Earn the final table through a choice or resolved question.** The closing at 09:30 is thematically sound, and C18 rightly preserves human agency. Its cooperation language remains generic. A brief return to an established service/community task can show what cooperation preserves, with bounded illustrative scope. The table then represents a chain the viewer has seen functioning or threatened. Avoid implying guaranteed starvation or guaranteed recovery; make the ending answer what the dependencies are and why choices matter.
8. **Close with a new perspective grounded in earlier evidence.** Lituya returns to a constraint and decision; without-humans reframes permanence after showing the maintenance/decay mechanism. Our 09:00 planet reality-check plus 09:30 prevention statement is sensible but mostly conceptual. Tie the reality-check to a specific surviving object/service already seen, then finish with the transformed meaning of the opening image. A call to action, if wanted, can ask about that real dilemma rather than add a generic subscription paragraph.

These are screenplay and shot-design improvements for the portable style, not a request for paid retakes or a claim of guaranteed retention. They should be planned before generation, using independent sequence work and the existing fast production pipeline. Historical reconstruction, counterfactual illustration and deep-time speculation require different evidence labels. Do not inherit the reference’s short flash fragments, unsourced diagram values, cast inconsistency, overstated prediction dates or continuity/physics artifacts.
