# Delivery defaults on every device

Use 720p or the selected provider's 768p output, normally native 24 fps. Ask before
1080p; do not silently upscale. Ordinary shots last at least two seconds, including
internal generated cuts. Fill the landscape frame without black bars, portrait
footage, Shorts, blurred sides or padding; preserve important action when cropping.

Save the finished regular MP4 directly in the current user's Downloads directory.
On the owner's Mac that is `/Users/lakshmansai/Downloads/`; on another machine
resolve the receiving user's home/Downloads rather than using this literal path.
Keep working projects and resumable media on available project/SSD storage. Never
overwrite an unrelated export. A supplied voiceover's original filename is the
export/upload filename, replacing only its audio extension with `.mp4`.

Keep source-audio rules distinct: observational documentaries use native work
sound and no added score/narrator; AI movies and narrated travel retain their own
audio contracts; supplied-voiceover documentaries preserve the supplied voiceover.
No blanket sound rule overrides a selected style or explicit current instruction.

Preview muted in the background and pause/close immediately afterward. Exported
audio remains intact. Do not open a Drive video preview unless already muted.
When public Drive delivery is requested, verify completed upload, exact filename
and byte size, and Anyone-with-link Viewer permission. Sharing is not verified by
an upload toast alone. Do not send Discord/email messages without authorization.

Report elapsed phases, actual models, resolution, paid corrections versus reused
work, additional credits and verification limits. Estimates and account holds are
not invoices. Public delivery and technical checks are not owner acceptance.
