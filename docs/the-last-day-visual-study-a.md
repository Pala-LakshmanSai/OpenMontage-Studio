### Specific strengths and avoidable defects

The strongest reusable technique is **scale alternation**: wide source, material mechanism, vulnerable infrastructure, individual consequence, then wider system. Maps and diagrams serve a relationship that ordinary scenery cannot readily show. The cascade visibly supplies new subjects; the film is not just twenty minutes of the same flood.

The film also contains quality limitations that should not become style requirements. At the 14:40 sample an official-looking report has a malformed disease heading; at 15:10 a newspaper has mangled text; other monitor tables/charts are visually plausible but not independently substantiated. Glowing lightning-like paths appear over terrain. Cinematic people and official/research scenes are present despite the publisher description's statement about reconstruction footage. Their actual production provenance is unverified; do not label them authentic documentary evidence or claim this study proved which generator made them. The source opening also contains a narrow dark lower strip/interface area.

Future production should keep the explanatory function while using accurate, readable graphics and clearly illustrative reconstructions. No fabricated quotation, official document, casualty figure, current event or supposed scientific measurement should acquire factual authority from a convincing render. A caption claiming a figure is sourced is not itself verification.

### Visual treatment and improvement target

This reference favors dark blue/teal water and space, restrained amber light, volumetric atmosphere, large clear silhouettes and broad depth. The human frame is a scale witness rather than the main host. There is little visible typography in the inspected frames; explanatory meaning often comes from the images plus narration. Do not add constant kinetic captions merely because this is a YouTube style.

Shots of a column/ring, globe/cloud metaphor, coastline and impact recur recognizably in the sampled timeline. Some may be reused assets; exact duplicate-file/source-range identity was not audited. Future films should use callbacks deliberately while avoiding filler repetition. The giant early wave is dramatic imagery; it should not silently contradict later qualifications about propagation, scale or distance. A physically impossible camera or scale metaphor may illustrate an idea, but should not masquerade as a quantitative simulation.

Alternate cosmic/geographic scale, local exposure, physical material, infrastructure and human-scale details. Keep actors/hosts incidental unless the title requires them, and keep any selected identities consistent when they recur.
