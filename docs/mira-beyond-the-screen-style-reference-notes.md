## Observed visual techniques

- Third-person dramatic fiction dominates the popular sci-fi subset. Selfie reaction coverage belongs to the theater upload and must not become a face quota for the sci-fi style.
- People and relationships remain the visual center even in very large worlds. Ordinary meals, tools, cabins and travel precede and follow spectacular changes. Medium relationship coverage and close reactions connect establishing views to personal stakes.
- Worn industrial craft, practical buttons, dense cabins, service lights and physical cases contrast with smooth alien architecture, translucent biological forms and enormous spaces. This contrast changes with location rather than imposing one global teal/orange grade.
- Warm cabin/service light versus cold exterior/space light recurs, but Earth We Lost's purple forest and Meridian's green refuge demonstrate palette flexibility.
- Environmental scale and geography are shown in wide views; operational decisions use faces, hands, controls and inserts. The frames support this coverage pattern; exact focal lengths cannot be recovered from them.
- Sparse place/date/title treatments and a small MIRA watermark exist in the references. Ordinary sampled dramatic coverage generally lacks large explanatory overlays. Reproduction must use original branding; no copied watermark or automatic subtitles.
- Full finished films show recovery, reorientation, relationships and emotional aftermath after the danger. Episode endings can retain a larger unresolved question. A complete sample must close its own dramatic problem.

## Five-aspect production vocabulary

**Subject:** Original adult protagonist, clearly differentiated companion/support roles; people remain meaningful at wide scale. Do not clone Mira, PIP or another creator character.

**Subject Motion:** Goal-directed look, choice, contact, operation, result and reaction. Preserve completed action and persistent cost. Exact action-speed distributions remain unmeasured.

**Scene:** Lived-in mechanical interiors, hostile exteriors and contrasting alien places; diegetic screens/communications are distinct from overlay captions. Time/light progression needs a cause.

**Spatial Framing:** Establishing wide, medium relationships, close reactions and useful operational inserts. Preserve travel routes and object/cast positions; do not fabricate lens statistics.

**Camera:** Third-person cinematic coverage is observed. Specific movement/speed/lens claims need moving-passage inspection. Static frames cannot distinguish dolly motion, a locked camera, or digital reframing reliably.

## Implementation synthesis

The strongest current hypothesis is a character-led sci-fi mystery/adventure: a concrete personal question draws an ordinary capable person into a huge unfamiliar system; practical discoveries change the plan; trust and attachment make the large stakes matter; the ending answers the personal question and shows a lasting change. Preserve space for domestic warmth, mild banter, discovery and aftermath. Do not reduce it to constant catastrophe, generic narrator-led exposition, a color palette or an alien-location slideshow.
